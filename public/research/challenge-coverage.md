# Problem-statement coverage and remaining work

This is a decision-support prototype, not a complete operational procurement system. Features marked **prototype** depend on synthetic data or unvalidated assumptions; do not use them as verified forecasts or instructions to buy.

| Problem-statement area | Available now | Remaining work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Budget, opportunities and quantities | Prototype inventory planner ranks hotel/flight product-periods and makes budget-constrained quantity recommendations. Supplier minimums and one-time fees can be entered manually. | Reliable demand and costs, supplier-wide constraints, currencies, taxes, deposits, shared capacity and an independently validated optimizer. |
| Bookings, searches, look-to-book, sold-outs, seasonality | Manual/CSV fields feed an illustrative formula; historical validation and public industry-traffic studies are separate. | Real, timestamped TBO product-level histories, censoring correction for sell-outs, leakage-free rolling holdouts and validated demand models. |
| Destinations, periods and selling prices | Prototype compares product-periods and five assumed prices, with rule-based timing and quote expiry. | Calibrate price response and timing on representative data; model customer substitution and actual future availability. |
| External signals and flight prices | Travel data, cached fare offers, weather and news are shown as context; historical forecast experiments and quality gates are available. | Verify data rights, freshness and cost; test whether each external signal improves representative out-of-sample forecasts. Cached fares are not supplier offers. |
| Cancellations, demand and price changes, unsold risk | Portfolio stress tests, unsold recovery assumptions and a separate fixed-booking cancellation sandbox. | Calibrated cancellation/no-show probabilities, customer and supplier refund rules, timing, correlated shocks, liability and real booking histories. |
| Supplier quotes and pre-buy | Manual browser-only quote review and manual supplier assumptions; illustrative pre-buy/flexible-supply suggestions. | Authorized TBO/supplier credentials, actual prices and availability, contract terms, secure approval, order placement, reconciliation and monitoring. No purchases are made. |
| User access and operations | Account-scoped private plans and private event drafts/rooms coexist with the original shared dashboard. | Legacy event/booking APIs and guest links remain shared by explicit choice; secure migration with a user-approved access transition, verified ownership, audit and production security review. |

Priority: (1) protect legacy event/booking data without breaking existing guest links, with an agreed migration; (2) integrate representative dated booking, search, cancellation and supplier histories and validate models; (3) extend real contract and cost handling; (4) connect supplier APIs only when access is available, behind explicit human approval. Public data can support experiments, but cannot substitute for TBO-specific data or verified supplier agreements. Provider free tiers and licensing restrictions must be checked before operational use.
