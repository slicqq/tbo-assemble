# Problem-statement coverage and remaining work

This is a decision-support prototype, not a complete operational procurement system. Features marked **prototype** depend on synthetic data or unvalidated assumptions; do not use them as verified forecasts or instructions to buy.

## Assessment against the original TBO challenge

**4/10 overall alignment; 6/10 as a hackathon prototype; 2/10 for real purchasing.** These are qualitative engineering judgments, not measured accuracy or profitability. The official DGCA workbook bridge improves traceable route evidence, but does not supply TBO booking/search/look-to-book histories or supplier offers, and the September 2026 counts remain stale under the forecast gate in November. Therefore it does not materially change the earlier overall rating. The quote checklist is not an authorized approval system; account-scoped review snapshots record user-generated comparisons, and no supplier purchase is placed. Private evidence has been exercised with real PostgreSQL and a local authentication double; a real signed-in save/reload still requires confirmation. Existing shared legacy event and guest access remains active by explicit user choice.

**Update, 16 Nov 2026 (portfolio risk engine `risk-cvar-v5`):**

- **What changed:** the workspace now plans on correlated seasons with CVaR, chooses between pre-paid and flexible allotment units, recovers demand lost to sell-outs, and recommends buy windows. On the demo inputs and the same 2,000 out-of-sample seasons, it beats v4 on both expected profit (₹22.6 L vs ₹20.2 L) and worst-10% profit (₹12.3 L vs ₹10.8 L), with less capital at risk. Its plans are certified within 0.01% of a proven upper bound.
- **What did not change:** the inputs are still synthetic and no supplier is connected.
- **Revised qualitative judgment:** 5/10 overall alignment; 7/10 as a hackathon prototype; still 2/10 for real purchasing. See [risk-engine.md](risk-engine.md).
